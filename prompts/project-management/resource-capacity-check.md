# Resource Capacity Check

See who is overloaded and who has room.

## Prompt

```text
Use this team capacity sheet to compare assigned hours to available hours, calculate utilization for each person, and flag anyone above 90% utilization or below 50%.
```

## Sample data

- `../../../datasets/project-management/team-capacity.csv`

## Expected result

- Calculates utilization per person
- Flags overloaded and underused team members
- Creates a capacity summary for planning
- Helps rebalance assignments
