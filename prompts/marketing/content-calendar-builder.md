# Content Calendar Builder

Turn a flat content backlog into a usable editorial calendar.

## Prompt

```text
Organize this content backlog into a calendar by publish date, channel, owner, and status. Add a weekly view, highlight overdue items, and group work by campaign.
```

## Sample data

- `../../../datasets/marketing/content-backlog.csv`

## Expected result

- Structures the backlog into a cleaner calendar layout
- Highlights overdue or blocked items
- Groups content by campaign
- Makes owner and status easy to scan
