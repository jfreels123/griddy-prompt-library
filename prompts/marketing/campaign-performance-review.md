# Campaign Performance Review

Summarize campaign results and identify what is underperforming.

## Prompt

```text
Analyze this campaign performance table, calculate CTR and conversion rate where needed, sort campaigns by spend efficiency, and highlight the campaigns that have high spend but low conversion.
```

## Sample data

- `../../../datasets/marketing/campaign-performance.csv`

## Expected result

- Calculates missing performance metrics
- Sorts campaigns by efficiency
- Flags high-spend, low-conversion campaigns
- Produces a simple decision-ready summary
