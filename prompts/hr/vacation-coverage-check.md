# Vacation Coverage Check

See where time off creates staffing risk.

## Prompt

```text
Review these time-off requests, calculate overlapping absences by team, and highlight any dates where coverage drops below two people available. Add a manager summary by week.
```

## Sample data

- `../../../datasets/hr/time-off-requests.csv`

## Expected result

- Finds overlapping absences
- Flags low-coverage dates
- Summarizes coverage risk by week
- Gives managers a quick approval view
