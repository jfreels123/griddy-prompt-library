# Headcount Plan Vs Actual

Compare staffing plan to actual hires.

## Prompt

```text
Compare planned headcount to actual headcount by team and month, show the gap, and flag the teams that are behind plan by more than one role.
```

## Sample data

- `../../../datasets/hr/headcount-plan.csv`

## Expected result

- Compares plan vs actual by period
- Shows hiring gaps by team
- Flags teams behind plan
- Produces a concise operating summary
