# Cash Runway Estimate

Estimate how long current cash lasts under the current burn pattern.

## Prompt

```text
Use this monthly cash and burn table to calculate current runway in months, show the ending cash balance for each month, and flag the month where cash drops below zero.
```

## Sample data

- `../../../datasets/finance/cash-runway.csv`

## Expected result

- Calculates monthly ending cash
- Calculates approximate runway in months
- Flags the month where cash turns negative
- Produces a simple management-ready summary
