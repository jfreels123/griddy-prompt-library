# Wedding Budget Overruns

Keep a wedding budget under control without manual math.

## Prompt

```text
Compare planned and actual wedding costs by category, show how much each category is over or under budget, and highlight the vendors with the biggest overruns. Add a total remaining budget figure.
```

## Sample data

- `../../../datasets/personal/wedding-budget.csv`

## Expected result

- Calculates category-level over/under budget
- Highlights major overruns
- Calculates remaining budget
- Produces a clean planning summary
