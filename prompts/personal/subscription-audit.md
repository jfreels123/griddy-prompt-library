# Subscription Audit

Find recurring costs that should be reviewed.

## Prompt

```text
Review this subscription list, total monthly and annual recurring spend, flag subscriptions that have not been used recently, and identify the most expensive subscriptions by category.
```

## Sample data

- `../../../datasets/personal/subscriptions.csv`

## Expected result

- Totals recurring spend
- Flags stale subscriptions
- Sorts services by cost
- Gives a simple cancellation shortlist
