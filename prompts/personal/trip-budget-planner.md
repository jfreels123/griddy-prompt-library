# Trip Budget Planner

Turn travel cost estimates into a working trip budget.

## Prompt

```text
Build a trip budget from this travel table. Group costs by category, compare planned versus booked spend, calculate the remaining budget, and highlight the categories that are trending over plan.
```

## Sample data

- `../../../datasets/personal/trip-budget.csv`

## Expected result

- Totals trip costs by category
- Compares planned and booked amounts
- Calculates remaining budget
- Flags categories trending over plan
